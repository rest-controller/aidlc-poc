# Stakeholder Map

## Key Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest | Authority | Source |
|-------------|----------|-----------|--------|
| Developer (POC owner) | Understanding AI-DLC's workflow end-to-end; building a working login feature | Decision-maker — owns scope, priority, and technical choices | [Q3] |
| Pallav (colleague) | Observing and evaluating AI-DLC's structured development approach | Influencer — reviews outcomes, provides feedback | [Q3] |

## Decision-Makers vs. Influencers

- **Decision-maker**: The developer running the POC decides scope, priority, and technical direction. [Q3]
- **Influencer**: Pallav observes the process and outcomes but does not own decisions. [Q3]

## Communication Requirements

Informal, ad-hoc communication between two people on a small POC. No reporting cadence or formal communication channels needed. [Q3]

## Assumptions & Open Questions

None.
